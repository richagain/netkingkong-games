# Shanhai Tactics – Support

Questions, bug reports or purchase problems: **[zz@netkingkong.com](mailto:zz@netkingkong.com)**
Please include your device model, iOS version and what happened. A screenshot helps.

## What is free, and what does the purchase unlock?
The tutorial and the whole Southern Mountains region (19 stages) are free. One in-app purchase unlocks the Western Mountains, Beyond the Seas and the Finale (41 stages) for good. There are no ads, no subscription and nothing else to buy.

## I bought the unlock but stages are still locked / I reinstalled the game
On the title screen, tap **Restore Purchase** (bottom-right). Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it. The unlock screen has the same button.

## Where is my progress saved?
On your device only. Deleting the game deletes your progress; the purchase itself can always be restored.

## How do I change the language?
Open **Menu** and tap **Language**. The game supports English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese and Japanese.

## Which devices are supported?
iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later.

## Privacy
The game collects no personal data. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy-en.html).
