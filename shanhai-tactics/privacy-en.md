# Shanhai Tactics – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Shanhai Tactics ("the game") is made by Net Kingkong Games ("we", "us"). This policy covers the game on iPhone and iPad.

**In short: the game does not collect any personal data, and nothing about you is sent to us or to anyone else.**

## What is stored on your device
The game keeps a few files in its own storage on your device:
- your progress (current run, Bestiary, stars, bound beasts, unlocked squads);
- your settings (language, sound, text size);
- whether the full game has been unlocked.

These files never leave your device through the game, and we cannot see them. Deleting the game deletes them. If you use iCloud or computer backups, iOS may include them in your backup under Apple's terms.

## Purchases
The one-time unlock is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment and your Apple Account; we do not receive your name, email address or payment details. To decide whether to unlock the extra stages, the game asks Apple's StoreKit on your device whether the unlock has been purchased. Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase itself.

## What the game does not do
- No accounts or sign-in.
- No advertising.
- No analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind, and no third-party code that collects data.
- No network connection, apart from Apple's StoreKit for the purchase.

## Children
We do not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
